What RedotPay is, for anyone new to it

RedotPay is a crypto-friendly payment platform built around virtual and physical cards that let you spend digital assets like a normal bank card. Instead of converting crypto to cash yourself, RedotPay handles that conversion at the point of sale.

It supports a range of cryptocurrencies, works across many countries, and is managed entirely through its mobile app — card issuing, top-ups, and spend limits included.

Common questions

Do I lose the bonus if I forget to enter the code?

Usually yes — referral codes typically have to be entered during the original sign-up step and can't be added retroactively, so enter it before you submit your registration.

Is there a cost to using a referral code?

No. Entering a referral code costs nothing extra; it simply links your new account to an existing user and unlocks the new-user bonus.

Can I use RedotPay outside my home country?

RedotPay is built for cross-border spending, but card availability and supported regions can change, so check current country support in the app before relying on it for travel.
